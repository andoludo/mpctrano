    Workflow

    mpctrano sits downstream of Trano. The building description (house.yaml) stays the single source of truth: Trano renders it into Modelica models with its FastSmoothBuildings library, mpctrano calibrates them on monitoring data, estimates their state and controls the systems of the building.

     house.yaml ──trano (FastSmoothBuildingsMPC)──▶ building_mpc ──rumoca──▶ CasADi ODE  xdot = f(t, x, u, p)
            ──trano (FastSmoothBuildings)─────▶ the same building with the controllers of the YAML (rule-based reference)

    1. Model

    from mpctrano import BuildingModel

model = BuildingModel.from_yaml("house.yaml")                       # calls Trano (FastSmoothBuildingsMPC library)
model = BuildingModel.from_file("house.mo")                         # or the package of `trano create-model house.yaml FastSmoothBuildingsMPC`
closed_loop = BuildingModel.from_yaml("house.yaml", library="FastSmoothBuildings")  # the controllers of the YAML

    The FastSmoothBuildingsMPC model is close to the Buildings library: layered walls and floors, windows with the angular optics of their glazing, convection and long-wave exchanges in the room, infiltration, the hydronic circuits of the YAML (radiators, valves, mixing valves, pumps, boilers, air-to-water heat pumps and their buffer), ventilation units with heat recovery and PV arrays. The controls of the systems are inputs. BuildingModel reads everything from the Modelica model:

    • control_names - the inputs with a min and a max: valve positions, mixing valves, boiler part load ratio or heat pump speed (between 0 and 1), the heat of ideal emitters [W];
    • disturbance_names - the weather channels (weather_inputs.TDryBul, .TBlaSky, .winSpe, .winDir, .HDirNor, .HDifHor, .HGloHor) and the internal gains per floor area of each occupancy model (<occupancy>_gai.QRad, .QCon);
    • comfort_states - the air temperature of each zone (<zone>.TAir);
    • meters - the energy the systems drew or produced since the start, states in kWh: EFue (fuel), EEle (electricity of a heat pump), EFan (fans), EPro (PV);
    • simulate(x0, controls, disturbances, dt, start_time) - CVODES simulation, controls held over each step; the model depends on the time of the year (the solar position is computed inside it);
    • with_parameters({...}), select_parameters(patterns), write(directory).

    The model is nonlinear and stiff (water nodes): the optimisation problems collocate it on Radau points (trano_optimization), which keeps them stable whatever the step. rumoca does not report the unit of array states (wall layers, radiator water): BuildingModel restores the kelvin on them so that the collocation scales and bounds them as temperatures.

    The variables are named after the elements of the YAML (space_001.TAir, boiler_001.EEle, externalwall_0.G[1]): BuildingModel.from_yaml generates the model without the containers Trano groups the elements in since 0.57 (network.model(include_container=False)), and BuildingModel.from_file strips their prefixes (envelope1., production1., ...) from a containerised model, as written by trano create-model. Both give the same model; tests/test_trano_contract.py checks it, with the translation of Trano's models by rumoca, against the installed Trano.

    The CasADi export of these models needs rumoca 0.10.3 (rumoca main up to commit c74cddf); uv sync builds it from source, see the installation section of the README. The collocation, the moving horizon estimation and the CasADi model come from trano-optimization (formerly trano.optimization).

    2. Disturbances

    WeatherForecaster.from_model(model) reads the TMY3 .mos weather file of the runnable model (a file of the same name next to the YAML) and the occupancy schedules of the YAML: the weather channels at the middle of each step, the black-body sky temperature computed as the Buildings weather reader does (from the dew point and the opaque sky cover), and the radiant and convective gains of each occupancy model. ForecastNoise adds lead-time dependent errors to the temperatures, the irradiance and the gains; DataForecaster replays recorded disturbances.

    4. Closed loop, scenarios and the rule-based reference

    run_closed_loop(controller, estimator, plant, forecaster, steps) runs the cycle measure → correct → forecast → solve → apply → predict against a ModelPlant (a BuildingModel playing the real building, with sensor noise) and returns a ClosedLoopResult with the trajectories and the ControlMetrics, read from the meters of the plant: fuel and electricity bought and sold, cost at the price of each carrier, capacity charge, comfort violation in K·h, peak electric power, share of the energy bought below the median price.

    run_rule_based(closed_loop, forecaster, start, steps, dt, comfort, tariff) simulates the closed-loop BuildingModel of the same YAML, with the controllers it describes (thermostats on the valves, the boiler or heat pump holding its supply temperature, the mixing valve its set point), costed and assessed the same way: the reference the MPC is compared with.

    Scenario bundles a tariff, a comfort specification, the MPC settings, the forecast noise and the estimator; run_scenario, run_scenario_rule_based and Project.run / Project.run_rule_based execute it; compare puts several runs side by side.

    The executed tutorial notebooks are in the Tutorials section of this site; their source is in tutorials/, and each one opens in Google Colab with its Open in Colab badge (06_your_building to edit the building itself).
