# Tutorials

Hands-on notebooks on the energy flexibility of buildings with economic model predictive control, from a Trano
YAML description to flexibility indicators. The pages of this section are the executed notebooks of
[`tutorials/`](https://github.com/andoludo/mpctrano/tree/main/tutorials): the figures are interactive, the
widgets of the live sections are not (open the notebook in JupyterLab for those, see the
[tutorials README](https://github.com/andoludo/mpctrano/blob/main/tutorials/README.md)).

The closed-loop runs the notebooks read come from a committed cache (`tutorials/cache/`), built by
`make cache`; the references are listed in
[`tutorials/references.md`](https://github.com/andoludo/mpctrano/blob/main/tutorials/references.md) and the
plan of the series in [`tutorials/PLAN.md`](https://github.com/andoludo/mpctrano/blob/main/tutorials/PLAN.md).
