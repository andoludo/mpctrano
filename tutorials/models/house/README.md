# house: generated models

Generated by `tutorials/scripts/generate_models.py house`; do not edit.

- `base`: cavity walls with 10 cm of insulation, floor heating, 7 kW heat pump, DHW tank
- `old`: partially filled cavity walls and single glazing (pre-insulation envelope), 10 kW heat pump
- `radiators`: high temperature radiators (70/55 degC) instead of floor heating
- `big_heat_pump`: oversized 12 kW heat pump (more power to shift)
- `pv`: base house with 30 m2 of PV
- `pv_battery_ev`: base house with PV, an 8 kWh battery and an EV charger

Each variant exists as `<variant>_r3c2` (control model) and `<variant>_r4c3` (plant).
