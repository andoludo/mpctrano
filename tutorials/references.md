Fluvius operates the Flemish digital meters and delivers quarter-hour data. Dynamic-price contracts for households in Flanders require a digital meter (quarter-hour readings); first dynamic contract in the VREG V-test in February 2021; uptake about 0.4 % of households (VREG price report, April 2025). **Status: in force (ToE, dynamic contracts); 2026 ToE release 3 adopted/planned** **[search-verified]**
